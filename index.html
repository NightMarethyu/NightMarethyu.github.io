About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Brigham Young University–Hawaii (December 2024, summa cum laude) with a passion for building software that solves real problems. My background spans IT infrastructure, technical analysis, and full-stack development—giving me a unique perspective on bridging technical systems with user-facing solutions.

Most recently, I worked as a Technical Analyst in the Admissions Department at BYUH, where I leveraged Python, SQL, and data visualization tools to build dashboards that drove decision-making across multiple departments. I developed scripts to streamline workflows, created multi-year marketing campaigns, and trained team members—all while learning to collaborate effectively in a data-driven environment.

I'm deeply interested in automation, systems design, and building tools that empower teams. Whether it's writing efficient Python scripts, designing interactive dashboards, or architecting clean, maintainable code, I approach every project with a focus on clarity and impact.

I spent two years in the Philippines as a missionary and have a strong grasp of Japanese and Tagalog.
